# HR Policy: Remote and Hybrid Work

**Document ID:** HR-002
**Category:** HR
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-20
**Owner:** People Operations

## Eligibility
Employees in good standing (no active performance improvement plan) are
eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval
and role requirements. Roles that require on-site presence (facilities,
hardware labs, front-desk operations) are excluded.

## Equipment
The company provides a standard remote work kit (laptop, monitor, headset)
to employees approved for regular remote work. Requests are made through the
IT Asset Management portal and typically fulfilled within 10 business days.

## Expectations
- Remote employees must be reachable during core hours (10:00-16:00 in their
  local timezone).
- Cameras should be enabled for external client meetings.
- Remote work does not change performance expectations or deliverable
  timelines.

## International Remote Work
Working from a country other than your contracted country of employment for
more than 20 consecutive days requires prior approval from People Operations
and Legal due to tax and employment law implications.

## Security Requirements
Remote employees must use company-managed devices and connect through the
corporate VPN when accessing internal systems, per the Data Security Policy
(SEC-001).
