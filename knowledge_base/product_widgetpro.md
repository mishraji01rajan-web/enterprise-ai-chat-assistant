# Product Datasheet: WidgetPro

**Document ID:** PROD-001
**Category:** Products
**Last Updated:** 2025-08-01
**Owner:** Product Marketing

## Overview
WidgetPro is our flagship workflow-automation product, aimed at mid-market
and enterprise operations teams. It allows teams to build no-code automation
pipelines that connect internal tools, approve workflows, and trigger
notifications.

## Editions
| Edition     | Price (per seat / month) | Seats included | Key limits                  |
|-------------|---------------------------|-----------------|------------------------------|
| Starter     | $19                       | up to 10        | 5 active workflows            |
| Business    | $49                       | up to 100       | 50 active workflows           |
| Enterprise  | Custom (contact sales)    | unlimited       | unlimited workflows, SSO, SLA |

## Key Features
- Drag-and-drop workflow builder
- 200+ pre-built integrations
- Role-based access control
- Audit log export (Business and Enterprise editions)
- SSO / SCIM provisioning (Enterprise edition only)

## Support Tiers
Business edition includes standard support (next business day response).
Enterprise edition includes priority support with a 4-hour first-response
SLA, as defined in the Support SLA Policy (SUP-001).

## Known Limitations
WidgetPro Starter does not support custom integrations via the API; this
requires Business edition or higher.
