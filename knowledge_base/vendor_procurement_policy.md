# Policy: Vendor Procurement

**Document ID:** POL-003
**Category:** Policy / Finance
**Last Updated:** 2025-02-14
**Owner:** Procurement

## Vendor Onboarding
New vendors must complete a due-diligence questionnaire and, for any vendor
that will process customer data, a security assessment aligned with the
Data Privacy Policy (SEC-001) before a contract is signed.

## Purchase Approval Thresholds
- Under $1,000: department manager approval.
- $1,000-$25,000: department head approval + Procurement review.
- Above $25,000: requires a competitive quote process (minimum 2 vendors)
  and CFO approval.

## Preferred Vendor List
Procurement maintains a preferred vendor list for common categories
(software, hardware, professional services). Purchases from non-preferred
vendors above $5,000 require an exception justification.

## Contract Renewal
Vendor contracts auto-renew unless cancelled at least 60 days before the
renewal date. Procurement reviews all contracts over $10,000/year for
renewal 90 days in advance.
