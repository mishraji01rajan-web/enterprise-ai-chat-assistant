# Policy: Refunds and Cancellations

**Document ID:** POL-002
**Category:** Policy / Finance
**Last Updated:** 2025-10-15
**Owner:** Finance / Customer Success

## Subscription Products
Customers on monthly subscription plans may cancel at any time; cancellation
takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. No partial-month
refunds are issued for monthly plans.

Customers on annual subscription plans may request a pro-rated refund for
unused months within the **first 90 days** of the annual term. After 90
days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable except as required by law.

## One-Time Purchases (Product Licenses)
One-time product license purchases may be refunded in full within **14 days**
of purchase if the license has not been activated. Once activated, license
purchases are refundable only in the case of a verified product defect,
subject to Support engineering confirmation.

## Invoice Disputes
Customers disputing an invoice must submit the dispute in writing within 30
days of the invoice date. Disputed amounts are excluded from overdue-balance
calculations while the dispute is under active review, but undisputed
portions of the same invoice remain due on schedule.

## Refund Processing
Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days to the original
payment method. Refunds cannot be issued to a different account or payment
method than the original charge.
