# HR Policy: Annual Leave and Time Off

**Document ID:** HR-001
**Category:** HR
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-05
**Owner:** People Operations

## Overview
All full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at a rate of 1.75 days per
month, totaling 21 days per calendar year. Part-time employees accrue PTO on
a pro-rated basis according to their contracted hours.

## Accrual and Carryover
- PTO accrues monthly and is credited on the last business day of each month.
- Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following
  calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31st unless
  local law requires otherwise.
- New hires begin accruing PTO from their start date; there is no waiting
  period.

## Requesting Leave
1. Submit a leave request through the HR portal at least 5 business days in
   advance for planned leave.
2. Your manager must approve the request before it is considered confirmed.
3. Leave requests of 2 weeks or longer require an additional approval from
   the department head.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO and is not capped. Employees should notify
their manager as soon as reasonably possible. A doctor's note is required for
absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year, published annually on the
HR portal calendar. Employees working on an observed holiday are entitled to
a substitute day off within 30 days.

## Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave of absence may be granted at management discretion for periods
up to 90 days. Requests must be submitted in writing to People Operations at
least 30 days in advance except in emergencies.
