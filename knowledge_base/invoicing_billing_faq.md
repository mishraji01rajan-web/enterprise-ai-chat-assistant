# FAQ: Invoicing and Billing

**Document ID:** FIN-001
**Category:** Invoices / Finance
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-01
**Owner:** Finance

## How are invoices generated?
Invoices are generated automatically at the start of each billing cycle
based on active subscriptions and any one-time charges incurred during the
prior period (e.g., overage fees, professional services).

## What payment methods are accepted?
We accept ACH bank transfer, wire transfer, and major credit cards for
invoices under $10,000. Invoices at or above $10,000 must be paid by ACH or
wire transfer.

## Can invoice due dates be extended?
Due date extensions require Finance approval and are generally only granted
to customers with an active payment plan or a documented dispute (see
Refund and Cancellation Policy, POL-002). Extensions are noted directly on
the customer account.

## Who do I contact about a billing question?
Billing questions should be routed to Support, who will create a ticket
tagged "billing" for Finance follow-up if the question cannot be answered
directly using account data.

## What does "outstanding" mean on an invoice?
An invoice is "outstanding" (or "overdue") if its status is not `paid` and
the current date is after the invoice's due date. Invoices with status
`pending` before the due date are not overdue.
