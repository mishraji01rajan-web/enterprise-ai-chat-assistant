# Policy: Customer Payment Terms

**Document ID:** POL-001
**Category:** Policy / Finance
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-10
**Owner:** Finance

## Standard Payment Terms
All invoices are issued with **Net 30** payment terms unless a customer's
contract specifies otherwise. The invoice due date is calculated as the
invoice date plus 30 calendar days.

## Payment Violation Thresholds
A customer account is considered **in violation of payment policy** when any
of the following is true:

1. One or more invoices are **more than 30 days past due** (i.e., unpaid more
   than 60 days after the invoice date).
2. The customer's total outstanding overdue balance exceeds **$5,000**.
3. The customer has **3 or more** invoices simultaneously overdue, regardless
   of amount.

## Consequences of Violation
- **Tier 1 (30-60 days overdue):** Automated reminder emails; account flagged
  for review.
- **Tier 2 (60-90 days overdue, or violation criteria above met):** Account
  is placed on **credit hold** — new orders require Finance approval before
  processing. Support tickets from the account are still handled normally.
- **Tier 3 (90+ days overdue):** Account escalated to Collections. Service
  suspension may be considered per the Master Services Agreement.

## Grace and Exceptions
Customers with an active, approved payment plan on file are exempt from
automatic credit holds as long as they remain current on the plan. Exceptions
must be documented by Finance in the account notes.

## Currency and Late Fees
Late invoices accrue a 1.5% monthly late fee on the outstanding balance,
applied on the first day of each month the invoice remains unpaid, unless
prohibited by the customer's regional contract terms.
