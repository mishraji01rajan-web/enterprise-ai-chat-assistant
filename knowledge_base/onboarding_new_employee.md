# Process: New Employee Onboarding

**Document ID:** HR-004
**Category:** HR
**Last Updated:** 2025-04-18
**Owner:** People Operations

## Before Day One
IT provisions a laptop, email account, and access badges 3 business days
before the employee's start date. People Operations sends a welcome packet
with benefits enrollment instructions.

## Day One
New hires attend a 2-hour orientation covering company history, the Code of
Conduct (HR-003), benefits overview, and IT security training. Managers
should schedule a 1:1 with each new hire on their first day.

## First 30 Days
- Complete mandatory security-awareness training (assigned automatically).
- Enroll in benefits within 30 days of the start date; otherwise enrollment
  is delayed until the next open enrollment period.
- Meet with the assigned onboarding buddy at least weekly.

## First 90 Days
New hires complete a 90-day check-in with their manager and People
Operations to review progress against role expectations.

## Systems Access
Access to internal systems (including the AI enterprise chat assistant) is
granted based on role. Access to customer financial data requires manager
approval and completion of the Data Privacy Policy (SEC-001) training
module.
