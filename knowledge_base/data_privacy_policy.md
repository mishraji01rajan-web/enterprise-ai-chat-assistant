# Policy: Data Privacy and Handling

**Document ID:** SEC-001
**Category:** Security / Policy
**Last Updated:** 2025-05-12
**Owner:** Information Security / Legal

## Classification
Customer data is classified as **Confidential**. Employee personal data is
classified as **Restricted**. Publicly published product information is
classified as **Public**.

## Access Controls
Access to Confidential and Restricted data requires role-based authorization.
Access requests are reviewed by the data owner and Information Security.
Standing access is reviewed quarterly; unused access is revoked.

## Data Retention
- Customer support tickets: retained for 3 years after closure.
- Invoices and financial records: retained for 7 years per regulatory
  requirements.
- Chat assistant conversation logs: retained for 1 year for quality and
  audit purposes, then anonymized.

## Third-Party Data Sharing
Customer data is never shared with third parties except as required to
deliver contracted services (e.g., payment processors) or by legal
obligation, and only under an executed data processing agreement.

## Incident Response
Suspected data breaches must be reported to Information Security within 24
hours of discovery. The incident response team will assess scope, notify
affected parties as required by law, and file a post-incident report within
30 days.
