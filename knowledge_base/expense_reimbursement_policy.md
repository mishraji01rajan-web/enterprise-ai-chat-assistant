# Policy: Expense Reimbursement

**Document ID:** HR-005
**Category:** HR / Finance
**Last Updated:** 2025-03-22
**Owner:** Finance

## Eligible Expenses
Reasonable travel, client-entertainment, and business-related expenses are
reimbursable when incurred for a legitimate business purpose and submitted
with an itemized receipt.

## Submission Window
Expenses must be submitted within **60 days** of being incurred. Expenses
submitted after 60 days require VP-level approval to be reimbursed.

## Approval Limits
- Under $500: manager approval only.
- $500-$2,500: manager + department head approval.
- Above $2,500: manager, department head, and Finance approval.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal entertainment, traffic fines, first-class airfare (unless flight
duration exceeds 6 hours), and alcohol beyond a reasonable amount at client
dinners are not reimbursable.

## Corporate Card
Employees issued a corporate card should use it for all eligible expenses.
Cash advances against the corporate card are not permitted.
