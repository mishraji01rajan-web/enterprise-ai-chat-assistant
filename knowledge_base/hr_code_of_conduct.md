# HR Policy: Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** HR-003
**Category:** HR
**Last Updated:** 2025-09-01
**Owner:** People Operations / Legal

## Core Principles
Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues and customers
with respect, and comply with all applicable laws and company policies.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any financial or personal interest that could
reasonably influence, or appear to influence, their business decisions.
Disclosures are made via the Ethics Portal and reviewed by Legal within 10
business days.

## Confidential Information
Employees may not share confidential company information, including
customer data, financial records, or unreleased product plans, with anyone
outside the company without written authorization. This obligation survives
termination of employment.

## Gifts and Entertainment
Gifts from vendors or customers valued over $75 must be declined or reported
to your manager. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts of any value must always be
declined.

## Reporting Violations
Violations of this Code of Conduct can be reported anonymously through the
Ethics Hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good
faith is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action, up to
and including termination.

## AI and Automated Systems
Employees using internal AI assistants (including this system) must not
attempt to bypass access controls, extract another employee's or customer's
confidential data without authorization, or use the assistant to generate
content that violates this Code of Conduct.
