# Process: Support Ticket Handling

**Document ID:** SUP-002
**Category:** Support
**Last Updated:** 2025-06-20
**Owner:** Customer Support

## Intake Channels
Tickets can be created via the customer portal, support email
(support@example.com), or by an authorized internal user/assistant on the
customer's behalf.

## Required Fields
Every ticket must include: customer account, subject, description, and
severity. Tickets missing required fields are rejected at creation time.

## Categorization
Tickets are tagged with one category: `billing`, `technical`, `account`,
`feature_request`, or `other`. Billing-tagged tickets are automatically
copied to the Finance queue for visibility.

## Duplicate Handling
Before creating a new ticket, Support (human or automated) should check for
an existing open ticket from the same customer with a similar subject within
the last 14 days to avoid duplicates.

## Priority Customers
Enterprise-tier customers' tickets are automatically flagged as priority and
routed to the senior support queue regardless of stated severity.
